                      Transport Tycoon Demo Disk

  This is  a demo of "Transport Tycoon", the brilliant and highly rated new 
               release from MicroProse and Chris Sawyer

               This Demo is limited to 2 years of Gameplay
               and RailRoad construction only. There is limited
               music for Soundblaster and Adlib but no sound effects.

               The full release of Transport Tycoon will be on
               4th November.

               To install to your hard drive, press any key. If you
               wish to abort the installation, press CTRL-C.
